UNextFuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

UNextFuel is a free Android app that shows fuel stations for trucks on a map. It is published by Eontar Technologies Ltd, United Kingdom ("we"). This page covers only UNextFuel.

1. What we collect and why

Your location. The app asks for permission to use your approximate and precise location. It uses it on your phone to show your position on the map and to keep the map centred on you. We do not store your location and do not send it to our servers while you browse the map. If you choose to report a diesel price, the app sends your position once, at that moment, so the server can check that you are close to the station (within 250 metres). Location permission is optional: without it the map still works, but your position is not shown.

Optional account (Sign in with Google). You can use UNextFuel without an account. If you choose Settings > Account > Sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and a unique account identifier. We use them only to identify your account and keep you signed in. We do not receive your Google password. We do not use the account to build a profile, and we do not sell or share the email address. The app does not synchronise or store your settings or locations on our servers. You can sign out or permanently delete your account at any time (section 4).

Diesel price reports (optional). When you report a price, we receive the station identifier, the price, the currency, a random anonymous device code generated by the app, and the position check described above. Reports are not linked to your account, name or email. The random device code is only used to limit repeat reports (one per station every 6 hours). Reports are kept without any information that identifies you. Prices shown in the app are the latest report from the last 7 days.

Settings on your phone. Language, theme and your cargo height are saved only on your phone.

Advertising. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob (two banners). To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID, your IP address and basic device information. Where the law requires it (UK, EEA, Switzerland), the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time: Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices (shown where Google requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Map tiles and navigation. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which can see your IP address when tiles are requested. The Navigate button hands the station position to your own navigation app, or opens a map in your browser.

2. What we do not collect

Without an account we collect no name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or payment data. With the optional Google account we hold only your email address and account identifier. We do not sell personal data.

3. How long we keep data

Location used for the map is not stored by us. Price reports are kept without identifying you. Your account (email address and identifier) is kept until you delete it. Advertising data is held by Google under its own policies.

4. Your rights and deleting your account

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data we hold about you, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. Your account and email address are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • If you cannot open the app, email deleteaccount@eontar.co.uk or see https://legal.eontar.co.uk/unextfuel/delete-account/. We delete the account within 30 days.
  • To withdraw ad consent, use Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices or your phone's advertising ID settings.
  • You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

5. Children

UNextFuel is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Reference only

Station and height information comes from OpenStreetMap contributors and user reports. It is for reference only, may be wrong or out of date, and is not official or guaranteed. The app does not check your route for low bridges or tunnels. Always follow road signs.

7. Changes

We will update this page and its date when something changes. Material changes will also be announced in the app description.
