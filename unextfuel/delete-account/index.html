UNextFuel: how to delete your account

Last updated: 9 November 2026

UNextFuel is published by Eontar Technologies Ltd. An account is optional; the app works fully without one.

Delete it in the app (immediately)

  1. Open UNextFuel > Settings > Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and email address are permanently deleted straight away.

Cannot open the app?

Email deleteaccount@eontar.co.uk with the subject "Delete my UNextFuel account", from the Google address you signed in with (or use the contact form on eontar.co.uk). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm.

What is deleted

The account record: your email address and account identifier. Station price reports are anonymous and are not linked to an account, so they stay without any information that identifies you. Settings stored on your phone are not affected.
